AI incident response that sounds like your senior teammate — not a generic chatbot.

Splunk-grounded team-aware prod & pre-prod
The problem

Before you can fix anything,
you go context-hunting.

An engineer gets paged. Then the ritual begins: Splunk → wiki → runbook → the code → Slack. Roughly 20 minutes of digging before reasoning even starts — and it's worse across team boundaries, where the knowledge is siloed.

“The hardest part of an incident is gathering context.”

What it does

One command. A grounded plan.
Right where you already are.

01

Monitor

Watches Splunk logs across production and pre-prod, live.

02

Detect

Flags application-breaking exceptions and promotes them to incidents.

03

Ground

Pulls the owning team's runbooks, past incidents & code.

04

Resolve

Summary + step-by-step fix — emailed to the team or on the dashboard.

The key idea

Same bug. Different team.
Different plan.
