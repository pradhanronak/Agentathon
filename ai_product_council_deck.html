Day-to-day workflow

  1. 01Paste the feature spec.
  2. 02Pick target segment and rollout.
  3. 03Run. Eleven agents return in ~30 seconds.
  4. 04Review the verdict and the ranked enhancement list.
  5. 05Iterate the spec before any sprint planning begins.

Roadmap to production

  • Workflow integrations
    Jira, Confluence, Notion, Slack — embed the Council where PMs already work.
  • Persona calibration per BU
    Ingest CSAT and survey data so retail, wealth, commercial, and cards each get tuned archetypes.
  • Governance & bias review
    Quarterly review by actual stakeholder leads. Persona-drift monitoring.
  • Outcome feedback loop
    Real launch metrics tune persona predictions over time. Council sharpens with use.
